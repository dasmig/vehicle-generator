vehicle-generator's data, in resources/, comes from these sources.

Derived by the scripts in scripts/ (see doc/data.md for each column):

- Fleet age (ages.tsv) for the countries Eurostat measures: Eurostat,
  road_eqs_carage (reuse with attribution, Commission Decision 2011/833/EU).
- Fuel mix of new cars (fuel_shares.tsv) in Europe from 2013: Eurostat,
  road_eqr_carpda; electric and plug-in shares elsewhere: Our World in Data,
  from the IEA Global EV Outlook, CC BY 4.0.
- Populations and driving side (countries.tsv): country-generator, which
  states its own sources.
- World manufacturer identifiers no maker holds (wmis.tsv): checked against
  NHTSA vPIC (US public domain).

Curated for this library from general knowledge, national registration
rules and statistics, the makers' specifications and VIN guides, rounded
public sales rankings and the paint makers' colour popularity reports;
nothing was copied from a dataset, so they are released under the MIT
licence of the library: cars per 1,000 people, mean fleet ages, markets,
make shares, models and trims, automatic shares, colours and colour names,
yearly mileage, plate formats, plate series anchors, plate region codes,
factories and their WMIs, owner factors, and the fuel mix anchors outside
the measured years.
